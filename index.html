About Us

We grew up watching the women we love hold families together.

Through sheer strength, intuition, and resilience. We saw the daily burdens that often go unseen — the mental load, the constant scheduling, the emotional balancing act, the "invisible work" that never stops.

And we also saw how rarely they ask for help.

What if technology could finally show up for moms the way they have always shown up for everyone else?

Built for the moments that matter most.

Additional Features

01

Female Cycle Tracker

Stay in tune with your body. Track your cycle, predict upcoming phases, and get personalized wellness tips timed to how you feel — all in one private, intuitive space.

02

Smart Gift Finder

Never stress over gift ideas again. Tell us who you're shopping for and your budget, and our AI curates thoughtful, personalised gift suggestions for every birthday, holiday, and milestone.

03

Scan & Schedule

Point your camera at any party invite, school poster, or flyer and we'll instantly extract the details — date, time, location — and add them directly to your calendar. No more missed events.

04

Mental Notes

Capture fleeting thoughts the moment they strike. Voice-record or type quick mental notes on the go — your AI organises them, surfaces the important ones, and quietly clears the mental clutter.

05

Shared Family Space

Bring the whole family together in one connected hub. Add your partner, kids, or caretakers, share calendars and reminders, and keep everyone on the same page — effortlessly.

06

Smart To-Do Lists

More than a checklist — your AI helps you prioritise tasks, suggests the best time to tackle them, and checks items off automatically when it detects you've completed them. Stay on top, stress-free.
