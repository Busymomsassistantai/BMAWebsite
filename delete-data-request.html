Delete Your Account

Request permanent deletion of your Busy Moms account and all associated data.

What gets deleted

  • Account and profile
  • Events, tasks, reminders, and contacts
  • Family members, recipes, and shopping lists
  • Life receipts and cycle data
  • Notification settings, sync mappings, and push tokens
  • Subscription record and all other associated app data

What stays

  • Events the app added to your Google Calendar remain on Google Calendar
  • Your Google Contacts and Google Tasks are not deleted
  • Items assigned to your email by other users are unlinked, not deleted

iOS subscriptions

If you have an active App Store subscription, you must cancel it yourself in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Apple does not allow us to cancel subscriptions on your behalf.

Timeline

Deletion is processed within 30 days of confirmation.
